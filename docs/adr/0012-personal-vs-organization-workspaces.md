# ADR-0012 — Personal teaching and organization supervision are separate projections

**Status: Accepted**

## Decision

A user may simultaneously be an organization owner/admin and a teacher. The clients must not merge those responsibilities into a single ambiguous Today/Students projection.

Personal workspace = the user's legal teaching responsibility.

Organization workspace = organization-wide management/supervision capability.

Management visibility never automatically changes Case owner/Action assignee or creates a StudentTeacherAssignment.
